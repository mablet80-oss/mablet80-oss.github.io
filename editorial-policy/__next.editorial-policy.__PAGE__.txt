3:T633,<h2 id="independence">Independence</h2>
<p>Editorial decisions are made by the editorial team alone. Clinics, manufacturers, and agencies do not review, approve, or pay for editorial content. Where a manufacturer document is used as a source it is labelled as such.</p>
<h2 id="authorship">Authorship</h2>
<p>Every page names its author. Where a page is produced with the help of research and drafting software, a human editor is responsible for the published version and for the sources cited.</p>
<h2 id="medical-review">Medical review</h2>
<p>Medical content is reviewed by doctors before publication. Reviewers check accuracy against the cited sources; they do not endorse any clinic or product. The doctors are named, with their credentials, in the methodology — not on each page, unless one of them has signed a specific page, in which case that page names them and gives the date.</p>
<p>Review is currently carried out by two doctors, Dr. Seungsun Yoo and Dr. Yeri Kim of Dongkyung Korean Medicine Clinic.</p>
<p>Pages that relay an official Korean rule — the Official Resources section — are not medical content. They cite the ministry, agency, or statute instead of naming a reviewer, and say so on the page.</p>
<h2 id="corrections">Corrections</h2>
<p>Errors are corrected on the page itself, with a note describing the change and the date. Material corrections that change the meaning of a Short Answer are also recorded in the page's update history.</p>
