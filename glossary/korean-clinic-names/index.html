Korean clinic names in English

Korean clinics list treatments under Korean names, and many of those names are not used anywhere else. Each entry gives the name as written in Korean, how to say it, what it is, and the names it goes by in English. Some names are a clinic’s own label for a mix it chooses, and those entries say so rather than name a product.

80 names. Each is linked to the source that shows the mapping.

Injections and threads

Toxins, fillers, boosters, threads and the house names clinics give to injections.

스킨보톡스seukin botokseuIn English: skin botox, microbotox, mesobotox, microdroplet botulinum toxin, intradermal botulinum toxin

Botulinum toxin injected as many tiny droplets into the skin (intradermal) instead of into the muscle.

Price lists sell it by area or by cc of diluted solution (e.g. full face 2cc), not by units, and the toxin brand varies by option.

Jeisys has no tip called a pore tip; it lists pores as a use of its S-series tips, and one listing sells "N팁 모공모드". Ask which tip 모공팁 means.

프락셀peurakselIn English: Fraxel, fractional laser

Fraxel is Solta Medical's fractional laser brand, but 프락셀 on Korean listings is used for fractional treatment on many devices.

Listings say 피코프락셀, 에어프락셀, 모자이크 3D 프락셀 and 모공프락셀, which are not Solta's Fraxel; ask which machine is used.

필링pillingIn English: chemical peel, peel

Usually a chemical peel: a solution (often an alpha- or beta-hydroxy acid) applied to the skin to exfoliate it, at superficial, medium or deep depth.

Listings also use -필 for many house-named skin-care steps (아쿠아필, 라라필, 블랙필) that are not all chemical peels.

Words on a price list

Words that change what a listed price buys.

눈앞개봉nunap gaebongIn English: opened in front of you, unsealed in your presence

Clinic promise to open a new, sealed product box in front of the patient before the treatment.

A clinic's own promise, not a regulated certification; some clinics do it only on request at booking.

Clinic listings: unni.app, unni.app

정품팁jeongpum tipIn English: genuine tip, authentic tip, original cartridge

Clinic phrase saying a device treatment uses the manufacturer's genuine tip or cartridge (e.g. Ultherapy, Thermage, Oligio).

Often paired with a promise that the shot count is shown to the patient; it is the clinic's claim, not an outside check.

Clinic listings: unni.app, unni.app

정품 / 정품정량jeongpum / jeongpum jeongnyangIn English: genuine product, genuine product, full dose

Clinic claim that the product is genuine (정품) and used at the stated dose (정량).

Appears on most toxin listings; it is the clinic's own claim, not an MFDS certification.

Clinic listings: unni.app, unni.app, unni.app

수면sumyeonIn English: sedation, IV sedation, sleep anaesthesia

Short for sedation (수면마취) offered with a procedure, as in 수면 울쎄라 (Ultherapy under sedation).

Whether the sedation fee is in the listed price varies: some listings say anaesthesia is included, others ask you to request sedation when booking.

Clinic listings: unni.app, unni.app

체험가cheheomgaIn English: trial price, intro price

Trial price: a discounted introductory price, usually limited to one use per person and one area.

Conditions such as 1인 1회 (once per person) and no stacking with other offers apply; the regular price is listed beside it.

Clinic listings: unni.app, unni.app

첫방문가 (첫방문)cheotbangmunga (cheotbangmun)In English: first-visit price, new-patient price

First-visit price: a discount only for a patient's first visit to that clinic, usually once.

Often combined with 체험가; later visits are charged at the regular listed price.

Clinic listings: unni.app, unni.app, unni.app

부가세 별도bugase byeoldoIn English: VAT excluded, plus VAT, excl. VAT

Price shown excludes VAT, which is added on top; Korean VAT law takes cosmetic procedures out of the medical-service VAT exemption.

Clinic websites may quote VAT-excluded prices while booking-app listings show VAT-included prices (VAT 포함), so compare like with like. The decree lists items such as wrinkle removal, skin regeneration and fat dissolution; toxin is not named by itself.
