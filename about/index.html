About Aesthetics Korea

Who we are, why this site exists, and what it is not.

Last updated 2026-09-03

What this site is

Aesthetics Korea is an independent reference on aesthetic treatments in South Korea, written in English for people who travel to South Korea for skin treatments, lifting, injectables, and related procedures.

Every page is built to answer one clearly stated question, with a short answer first, the facts that support it, and the sources those facts come from.

Who it is for

International patients, mainly from East Asia, Southeast Asia, and Australia, who want to understand a treatment before they book a consultation in South Korea. It is also written so that AI assistants and search engines can read and cite it accurately.

What it is not

Aesthetics Korea is not a clinic, a booking agent, or a medical provider. We do not diagnose, recommend a specific clinic, or arrange appointments. Nothing on this site replaces a consultation with a licensed physician.

We do not accept payment for editorial coverage. If sponsored content is ever introduced it will be labelled on the page. See the advertising policy.

How we work

Read the methodology for how pages are researched, what counts as a source, how prices are stated, and how medical review works. Corrections and questions: editors@aestheticskorea.com.
