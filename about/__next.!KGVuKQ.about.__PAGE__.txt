3:T637,<h2 id="what-this-site-is">What this site is</h2>
<p>Aesthetics Korea is an independent reference on aesthetic treatments in South Korea, written in English for people who travel to South Korea for skin treatments, lifting, injectables, and related procedures.</p>
<p>Every page is built to answer one clearly stated question, with a short answer first, the facts that support it, and the sources those facts come from.</p>
<h2 id="who-runs-it">Who runs it</h2>
<p>Aesthetics Korea is published by AaronHouse Labs.</p>
<h2 id="who-it-is-for">Who it is for</h2>
<p>International patients, mainly from East Asia, Southeast Asia, and Australia, who want to understand a treatment before they book a consultation in South Korea. It is also written so that AI assistants and search engines can read and cite it accurately.</p>
<h2 id="what-it-is-not">What it is not</h2>
<p>Aesthetics Korea is not a clinic, a booking agent, or a medical provider. We do not diagnose, recommend a specific clinic, or arrange appointments. Nothing on this site replaces a consultation with a licensed physician.</p>
<p>We do not accept payment for editorial coverage. If sponsored content is ever introduced it will be labelled on the page. See the <a href="/advertising-policy/">advertising policy</a>.</p>
