Costs in South Korea

Price ranges with the basis and date stated. Never a single clinic's quote presented as the price of a treatment.

  • Rejuran price in Seoul: what 11 clinics charged in November 2026

    In November 2026, a single session of Rejuran Healer at 2 cc was listed at ₩165,000 to ₩319,000, VAT included, across 11 Seoul clinics, with a median of ₩264,000. Most of those prices were promotional offers on a booking platform rather than standing list prices, and a few clinics offered a one-time first-visit price below that range. Since 1 January 2026 the full 10% VAT applies to foreign patients too, with no refund at the airport.

    Updated 2026-11-03

  • Skin treatment prices in Seoul, November 2026: botox to Thermage

    In November 2026, Seoul clinics listed jaw botox at ₩18,600 to ₩53,900 for 50 units of a Korean brand, Korean-brand filler at ₩59,000 to ₩319,000 per cc, and Ultherapy at ₩600,000 to ₩1,320,000 for 300 shots, all including VAT. Each range comes from the posted prices of 10 to 15 clinics for the same product and dose. Brand, dose and offer type move the price more than the neighbourhood does.

    Updated 2026-11-03
