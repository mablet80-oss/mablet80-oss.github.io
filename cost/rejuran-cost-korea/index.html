Rejuran price in Seoul, November 2026

On 3 November 2026 we read the posted price of Rejuran Healer at 2 cc, the usual volume for one session on the face, at 11 clinics in Seoul. The lowest was ₩165,000 and the highest ₩319,000, with VAT included. Half the clinics charged ₩264,000 or less.

The clinics were spread across Gangnam Station, Sinsa, Sinnonhyeon, Seolleung, Hongdae and Jamsil. Prices did not follow the neighbourhood: both the lowest and some of the higher prices were in the Gangnam and Sinsa area.

Where a clinic also listed 4 cc, the price was a little under double the 2 cc price. Three clinics listed it, at ₩286,000, ₩385,000 and ₩398,000.

What the listed price usually includes

The listed price covers the product and the injection for the volume stated. Several clinics bundled something else into the same price, such as a soothing mask, an LED session or a skin analysis, and said so on the listing. One clinic's price included a private treatment room.

Ask whether numbing cream and a consultation are included, and what else the quote covers, before you compare it with another clinic.

What changes the price

  • Volume. 2 cc and 4 cc are priced separately, so compare like with like.
  • Product line. Rejuran Healer is the standard product. Rejuran HB and Rejuran I (for the eye area) are separate products with their own prices.
  • Offer type. Most prices in this survey were promotional prices on a booking platform, many with an end date. Four clinics also offered a one-time first-visit price, the lowest at ₩150,000. Those offers are excluded from the range above because they apply once.

How the prices were collected

We read each price on the clinic's own listing on 강남언니 (Gangnam Unni), the booking platform most Seoul clinics use to post prices, or on the clinic's own website. Each row in the dataset gives the clinic, the area, the product, the volume, the price, whether it was a promotional or first-visit price, whether VAT was included, and the page it came from. The full dataset is linked in the sources below, so every figure on this page can be checked.

Platform prices in Seoul are posted with VAT included. Clinic websites often post prices before VAT, marked 부가세 별도. One clinic in this survey posted ₩240,000 before VAT, which we counted as ₩264,000.

We do not name clinics on this page and do not recommend any of them. A range from many clinics is more useful than any one clinic's quote, and prices on these listings change often. We check them again each quarter.

VAT and the tourist refund in 2026

Foreign patients could once claim back the VAT on cosmetic procedures at the airport. That refund applied to cosmetic medical services supplied up to 31 December 2025 and was not extended. From 1 January 2026, the 10% VAT is part of the price for everyone.

If a clinic or an older article still mentions the refund, it refers to treatment before 2026.

Checking the product and the bill

Korean clinics must post their prices for non-covered treatment, which includes all aesthetic treatment, and may not collect more than the posted amount for the same item. Keep a screenshot of the listing you booked from.

You can ask to see the box or syringe label before the injection. Rejuran is a licensed product in South Korea, and the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety's device portal lists what has been approved. The Rejuran treatment page covers what it is and what the studies found.

The bottom line

Rejuran Healer at 2 cc cost ₩165,000 to ₩319,000 including VAT at 11 Seoul clinics in November 2026, with a median of ₩264,000. Compare the same volume and the same product line, and treat first-visit prices as one-time offers. The 10% VAT is no longer refundable for procedures from 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Is the price per cc or per session?
Clinics in this survey listed Rejuran by the syringe volume used in one session, most often 2 cc for the face. Some also listed 4 cc, and some sold Rejuran together with soothing care or a skin analysis at one price. Ask which volume the quote is for before comparing two clinics.
Why do some Seoul clinics show a much higher crossed-out price?
Most listings show a higher crossed-out figure next to the price you actually pay. The figure you pay is the one that counts, and it is the one used on this page. A clinic may not charge more than the price it has posted for the same item.
Do foreigners pay more than Koreans?
The listings in this survey showed one price for everyone. Korean law requires clinics to post their non-covered prices and bars them from collecting more than the posted amount, so a higher quote should correspond to a different item or a larger volume.
Can I get the VAT back at the airport?
Not for procedures from 1 January 2026. The tourist refund for cosmetic medical services covered treatment supplied up to 31 December 2025 and was not extended.
