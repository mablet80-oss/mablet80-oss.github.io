22:T10c7,<h2 id="rejuran-price-in-seoul-november-2026">Rejuran price in Seoul, November 2026</h2>
<p>On 3 November 2026 we read the posted price of Rejuran Healer at 2 cc, the usual volume for one session on the face, at 11 clinics in Seoul. The lowest was ₩165,000 and the highest ₩319,000, with VAT included. Half the clinics charged ₩264,000 or less.</p>
<p>The clinics were spread across Gangnam Station, Sinsa, Sinnonhyeon, Seolleung, Hongdae and Jamsil. Prices did not follow the neighbourhood: both the lowest and some of the higher prices were in the Gangnam and Sinsa area.</p>
<p>Where a clinic also listed 4 cc, the price was a little under double the 2 cc price. Three clinics listed it, at ₩286,000, ₩385,000 and ₩398,000.</p>
<h2 id="what-the-listed-price-usually-includes">What the listed price usually includes</h2>
<p>The listed price covers the product and the injection for the volume stated. Several clinics bundled something else into the same price, such as a soothing mask, an LED session or a skin analysis, and said so on the listing. One clinic's price included a private treatment room.</p>
<p>Ask whether numbing cream and a consultation are included, and what else the quote covers, before you compare it with another clinic.</p>
<h2 id="what-changes-the-price">What changes the price</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Volume.</strong> 2 cc and 4 cc are priced separately, so compare like with like.</li>
<li><strong>Product line.</strong> Rejuran Healer is the standard product. Rejuran HB and Rejuran I (for the eye area) are separate products with their own prices.</li>
<li><strong>Offer type.</strong> Most prices in this survey were promotional prices on a booking platform, many with an end date. Four clinics also offered a one-time first-visit price, the lowest at ₩150,000. Those offers are excluded from the range above because they apply once.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="how-the-prices-were-collected">How the prices were collected</h2>
<p>We read each price on the clinic's own listing on 강남언니 (Gangnam Unni), the booking platform most Seoul clinics use to post prices, or on the clinic's own website. Each row in the dataset gives the clinic, the area, the product, the volume, the price, whether it was a promotional or first-visit price, whether VAT was included, and the page it came from. The full dataset is linked in the sources below, so every figure on this page can be checked.</p>
<p>Platform prices in Seoul are posted with VAT included. Clinic websites often post prices before VAT, marked 부가세 별도. One clinic in this survey posted ₩240,000 before VAT, which we counted as ₩264,000.</p>
<p>We do not name clinics on this page and do not recommend any of them. A range from many clinics is more useful than any one clinic's quote, and prices on these listings change often. We check them again each quarter.</p>
<h2 id="vat-and-the-tourist-refund-in-2026">VAT and the tourist refund in 2026</h2>
<p>Foreign patients could once claim back the VAT on cosmetic procedures at the airport. That refund applied to cosmetic medical services supplied up to 31 December 2025 and was not extended. From 1 January 2026, the 10% VAT is part of the price for everyone.</p>
<p>If a clinic or an older article still mentions the refund, it refers to treatment before 2026.</p>
<h2 id="checking-the-product-and-the-bill">Checking the product and the bill</h2>
<p>Korean clinics must post their prices for non-covered treatment, which includes all aesthetic treatment, and may not collect more than the posted amount for the same item. Keep a screenshot of the listing you booked from.</p>
<p>You can ask to see the box or syringe label before the injection. Rejuran is a licensed product in South Korea, and the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety's device portal lists what has been approved. The <a href="/treatments/rejuran/">Rejuran treatment page</a> covers what it is and what the studies found.</p>
